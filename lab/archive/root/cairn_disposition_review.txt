CAIRN DISPOSITION REVIEW - FINAL DETERMINATION
FROM: Cmdr. I. Petrov, ONI Section III
TO: Sealed Record (root/custodian access only)
DATE: 2547-02-19
RE: SPINDLE-era acquisition material - retention determination

This office was asked, at the close of the SPINDLE decommission, to
make a final determination on the acquisition-era material: destroy
it, or retain it. Disposition Order 2547-014 already states the
outcome. This file states the reasoning that order does not.

Every argument for destruction is operational. It is easier to manage
a program that cannot be found. Every argument against destruction is
not operational. It is that this office does not believe it has the
standing to decide, unilaterally, that the record of how this was done
should simply cease to exist because it is inconvenient to keep.

Determination: retained, not destroyed. Public disclosure deferred
indefinitely. No further declassification review is scheduled at this
time.

This office is not positioned to decide whether the people this
material describes are entitled to know their own history, or whether
the public is entitled to know what it cost to produce the people it
now calls heroes. That determination has not been made here, because
no mechanism exists to make it here. It has not been made anywhere
else either.

If a future office is ever positioned to make that determination, this
record should reach them intact, cross-references included. That is
the only instruction this file carries.

- I. Petrov
