CAIRN DISPOSITION REVIEW - FINAL SUMMARY
FROM: Disposition Review Office
TO: Sealed Record (root/custodian access only)
DATE: 2547
RE: SPINDLE / SPARTAN-II cross-reference, consolidated

This file exists to connect material that was deliberately kept apart
across systems. It should not be readable outside of a full disposition
review.

1. Origin (2517): Section III authorized a covert program, provisional
   designation SPARTAN-II, to remove a small cohort of colonial children
   and train them from early childhood as a counter-insurgency asset.
   See CAIRN record 103 (Halsey correspondence) and the acquisition
   directive on the confidential share.

2. Concealment: Each removal was covered by a substitute case closure -
   an accelerated-growth clone certified as a natural death - so that
   families and colonial administration would have no reason to
   investigate. See CAIRN record 102 (Medical Annex, Dr. M. Castel).

3. Outcome: Of the SPINDLE-era candidates cross-referenced in this
   review (Okafor, Wren, Farrow, Voight - see LEDGER case index and the
   backups-share casualty log), two did not survive augmentation, one
   was discharged with permanent disability, and two are recorded as
   entering active service. Their subsequent record is sealed above
   this office's clearance and is not reproduced here.

4. Program continuation: The cohort trained under this directive later
   became the personnel publicly known during the war against the
   Covenant as the SPARTAN-II program. Their service record during that
   war is public. Their origin, as described above, is not.

5. Disposition (Cmdr. I. Petrov, Section III, 2547): Material retained,
   not destroyed, "in case a future office is required to answer for it."
   No further guidance is given on when, or whether, that office should
   exist.

This office draws no conclusion here about whether that decision was
correct. It only confirms that the question was asked, on the record,
by the people who made it - and that the answer was to wait.
