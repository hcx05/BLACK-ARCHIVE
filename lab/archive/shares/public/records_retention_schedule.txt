RECORDS RETENTION SCHEDULE - REGIONAL DEPENDENT & PERSONNEL CASE FILES
ISSUED BY: K. Whitfield, Records Policy Office
REVISION: 2544-03 (supersedes 2538-11)

  CASE CATEGORY                          RETENTION PERIOD        DISPOSAL METHOD
  Active dependent case                  indefinite               n/a
  Closed dependent case (standard)       30 years post-closure    scheduled purge
  Closed dependent case (medical)        30 years post-closure    scheduled purge
  Transfer/disposition record            per originating order    per originating order
  Service account / system log           2 years                  automatic rotation

Notes: Cases are checked against this schedule whenever the holding or
originating system undergoes decommission, migration, or a scheduled
compliance sweep - review happens at the point something moves, not on a
fixed calendar cycle. Standard-category closures past the thirty-year
mark are flagged for purge at that time; flagged cases require sign-off
from Records Compliance before disposal, and unsigned flags roll over to
the next such review indefinitely. This has created a significant
backlog wherever a system goes a long time between migrations - Systems
has raised it at three consecutive quarterly meetings.

Disposition-hold records (see Disposition Order log) are exempt from
routine purge scheduling, but remain subject to custodial-status review
whenever the holding system is decommissioned or migrated, per standard
procedure.
