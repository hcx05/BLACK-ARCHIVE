CANDIDATE ACQUISITION DIRECTIVE - EXCERPT
FROM: ONI Section III
DATE: 2517
RE: Colonial counter-insurgency asset program (retained under Disposition Order 2547-014)

Background: Colonial administration risk projections (Section III,
2516 revision) indicate escalating probability of coordinated
insurrection across Outer Colony settlements within the decade. Standard
counter-insurgency response is judged insufficient to prevent
large-scale civil conflict.

Proposal: A small, genetically and cognitively screened cohort of
colonial minors will be identified, removed from their family units
without notification, and trained from early childhood toward specialized
counter-insurgency service. Removal will be concealed at the family and
colonial-administration level through substitute case closure (see
Medical Annex, CAIRN record 102).

This batch is acquired under the existing SPARTAN-II designation
(Section III internal use since 2511). Candidate screening against the
Outer Colony vaccination program's genetic records has been running
since the program's founding; this directive authorizes acquisition of
the seventy-five candidates selected from that screening pool,
beginning with this cycle.

This directive is not intended to be permanent doctrine. It is intended
to be survivable doctrine, for as long as the alternative is worse.
