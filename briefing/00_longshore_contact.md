That's the whole job. No org chart, no case number, no explanation for
why a stranger is paying (if they're even paying) an independent to look
into three dead six-year-olds from three colonies that have nothing to
do with each other.

You have three names, three colonies, and the fact that all three cases
were processed through the same category of records system. That's the
only thread in your hand.

Start there. See where it goes.
